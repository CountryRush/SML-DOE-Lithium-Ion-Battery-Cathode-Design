# Data sources and licenses

This repository contains code and a small simulated dataset. The raw external
datasets are not redistributed here; they can be obtained from the sources below.

## 1. Simulated dataset (this repository)

- `data/proposal_doe_dataset.csv` — 50-run computer experiment generated with
  PyBaMM 26.10.0.0 (DFN, Chen2020 parameter set). Generated by
  `code/run_proposal_doe.py`.

## 2. Warwick WMG calendering dataset (real-data validation)

- Faraji Niri, M.; Hidalgo, M.; Apachitei, G.; Dogaru, D.; Lain, M.;
  Copley, M.; Marco, J. *Data of Physical and Electrochemical Characteristics
  of Calendered NMC622 Electrodes and Lithium-ion Cells at Pilot-Plant Scale
  Battery Manufacturing.* Mendeley Data, 2023.
  DOI: **10.17632/wwhm2frfmy.1**
- License: **CC0 1.0**
- Companion papers:
  - Hidalgo et al., *Design of Experiments for Optimizing the Calendering
    Process in Li-Ion Battery Manufacturing*, J. Power Sources 573 (2023) 233091.
  - Faraji Niri et al., *The Impact of Calendering Process Variables on the
    Impedance and Capacity Fade of Lithium-Ion Cells*, Energy Technology 10
    (2022) 2200893.

Download: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/wwhm2frfmy/1

## 3. Mendeley NMC622 simulation dataset (literature comparison)

- Cho, H.-H. *Data on the effect of particle size, porosity and discharging
  rate on the performance of lithium-ion battery with NMC 622 cathode through
  numerical analysis.* Mendeley Data, 2021.
  DOI: **10.17632/vh5grc6y7f.1**
- License: **CC BY 4.0**

Download: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/vh5grc6y7f/1

## 4. Simulator

- PyBaMM (Python Battery Mathematical Modelling): https://www.pybamm.org/
- Chen et al. (2020), *Development of Experimental Techniques and Model
  Identification for Fast Charging of Lithium-Ion Batteries*,
  J. Electrochem. Soc. 167, 080534.
